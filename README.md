## FOIA Warfare

FOIA Warfare is an AI platform for Freedom of Information Act and Privacy Act requests, built for the people who file them.

Agencies are adopting AI to process requests. Requesters still work mostly by hand, one form and one agency at a time. FOIA Warfare is building the requester side: one system that takes a person from a question to a properly cited request, tracks the agency's statutory clock, handles the correspondence, and drafts the appeal when a response comes back wrong.

### What it is built to do

- **Intake and drafting.** A conversational analyst identifies the right agency and office, then drafts the request with the correct statutory citations, a fee waiver, and expedited processing language where it applies.
- **Filing.** Requests go out as signed letters, timed to reach agency FOIA offices at the start of their business day.
- **Correspondence.** The agent reads, routes and answers agency mail. The requester is asked only when a decision is theirs to make.
- **Deadlines and appeals.** Statutory deadlines are tracked, and an adverse determination gets an appeal drafted with its grounds and filing route.
- **Agency intelligence.** A maintained directory of federal FOIA offices and how each one handles requests.

It serves individual filers, attorneys and firms, enterprise legal and compliance teams, and press and watchdog organizations.

**Status:** in private build, with a waitlist open at [foiawarfare.com](https://foiawarfare.com). The source repository is private.
